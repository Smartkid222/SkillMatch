Section 3 / 13

The Solution

A unified AI-powered platform that screens, ranks and delivers only qualified candidates to recruiters.

1

AI CV Screening

Parses uploaded CVs, extracts skills, experience & education, then generates a relevance match score filtering out unqualified candidates instantly.

2

Skill-Based Tests

Shortlisted candidates complete a short AI-generated role-specific test covering technical ability and soft skills, producing a verified performance score.

3

Ranking Dashboard

Recruiters see candidates ranked highest to lowest by composite score (CV + test + AI match). One-click shortlist, reject, or move to interview.

4

AI Candidate Summary

Auto-generated recruiter-ready summaries highlight key strengths, experience gaps, suitability rating, and a hiring recommendation for each candidate.

Proven Result

Recruiter screening time reduced by 50% or more.

Section 5 / 13

Key Features

Core platform capabilities engineered across MVP and enhancement phases.

Job Posting Setup

Recruiters define job title, required skills, experience level and description. Becomes the reference for all CV screening.

CV Upload & Auto-Parsing

Applicants upload PDFs. System extracts skills, work experience and education to auto-populate a structured candidate profile.

AI Scoring Engine

Combines CV analysis, skill test score and job requirement matching into one composite ranking using context (not just keywords).

Skill-Based Tests

AI generates short role-specific assessments evaluating practical and soft skills, scored automatically and added to profile.

Recruiter Dashboard

Centralised ranked view of all candidates with score breakdowns, pipeline status, and quick-action shortlist/reject controls.

Collaboration Tools

Team members leave comments, share reviews and discuss candidates in-platform. Full audit trail supports collective decision-making.

How It Works

End-to-end workflow from job creation to final hiring decision.

1

Job Creation

Recruiter posts job with title, skills & description.

2

CV Upload

Candidates upload CVs (PDF); stored automatically.

3

AI Screening

AI analyses CVs for skill match, experience & alignment.

4

Skill Test

Shortlisted candidates complete role-specific AI assessment.

5

Scoring

Composite score: CV + test + AI match engine.

6

Ranking

Dashboard displays ranked candidates with score breakdowns.

7

Hiring Decision

Recruiter shortlists, rejects or moves to interview.

Competitor Analysis

Existing tools solve parts of the problem — not all of it.

Type Examples Strengths Weakness / Gap
Job Boards LinkedIn, Glassdoor Large reach, easy posting Manual screening required; keyword-driven with high irrelevant volume
ATS Systems Workday, Greenhouse, Lever Organises workflow, tracks candidates Still requires manual review; limited contextual understanding
AI Tools HireVue, Eightfold AI Automated scoring, reduces manual effort Expensive & complex; limited transparency in candidate evaluation
Freelance Hubs Upwork, Fiverr Skill-based, fast hiring Not suited for full-time structural recruitment

The SkillMatch Opportunity & Market Gap

"No affordable solution combines contextual AI matching + skill testing + transparent ranking in one single platform."
